I tested three prompt-engineering tasks—sentiment classification, product description generation, and data extraction—using repeated runs and iterative prompt revisions. Initial prompts produced inconsistent labels, unsupported product claims, and confusion between item and order identifiers. Explicit output constraints and schemas improved sentiment classification and extraction, while few-shot examples and additional grounding rules improved product descriptions and handling of ambiguous inputs. The final prompts passed the defined checks across 15 runs on each original input, although these results do not establish broad generalization or production reliability. Next time, I would define evaluation criteria earlier, provide richer verified product information, and reserve unseen test cases for final evaluation.
